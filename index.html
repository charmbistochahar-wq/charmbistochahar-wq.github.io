My Story

Hello, my name is Mohammad. I am an Iranian man going through one of the most difficult periods of my life.

The economic situation in Iran has become extremely challenging, especially for people with limited financial resources. Severe inflation and the rising cost of basic necessities have made it increasingly difficult to afford even the most essential things in everyday life.

I have experienced a serious financial crisis. I lost my source of income, I am currently unemployed, and I am struggling with significant debts. At the moment, I have no stable income and I am having a very hard time covering my basic living expenses.

I am not receiving financial support from any organization or institution, and I do not have health insurance to help me when I need medical care.

I am not seeking political support, and I do not represent any political group or ideology. I simply believe in peace and in the right of every person to live with dignity and hope.

I never imagined that I would have to ask strangers for financial help. But sometimes life puts us in situations where we cannot get through them alone.

I am asking for your help so that I can afford basic necessities such as food, healthcare, and other essential living expenses while I try to rebuild my life, find work, and become financially independent again.

Even a small donation can make a meaningful difference to me.

If you are able to help, please know that your kindness would mean much more to me than the amount itself. You would be helping another human being get through a very difficult chapter of his life.

Thank you sincerely for taking the time to read my story.

I am not asking for much. I simply want a chance to keep going and rebuild my life.

With gratitude,
Mohammad
